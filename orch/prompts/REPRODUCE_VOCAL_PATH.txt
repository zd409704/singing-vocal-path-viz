What to build
A dark three-column dashboard titled VOCAL PATH, synced to the demo singing clip:

1) Left: full-body wireframe side view with lungs, airway, and animated airflow; a lung-volume readout (clearly labeled illustrative).
2) Center top: midsagittal cut of the oral cavity and vocal tract (nasal cavity, soft palate / velum, hard palate, tongue, jaw, lips, pharynx, glottis) with illustrated airflow.
3) Center bottom: current syllable or phone (a grapheme plus a pronunciation line), current pitch name and cents offset, sliders or bars for tongue height, tongue backness, tongue tip, jaw opening, lip rounding, velum opening, and airflow, plus a small glottis schematic.
4) Right: piano-roll pitch chart (about G3 to D5), note blocks for detected sung notes, a live pitch trace that can wobble and show vibrato, color for recorded notes / stable tracking / pitch deviation, a breath or voicing energy strip under the roll, and a phoneme timeline. A playhead and timestamp scan left to right.

Keep the honesty already present in the source UI: notes come from the recording; articulation and airflow are illustrative, not physically measured. Extract pitch (and, if feasible, note segments and phones) from the demo audio. Drive tract and breath animation from a clearly labeled illustrative mapping (for example from estimated phones, pitch, and energy). Do not claim medical or studio-measured articulation.
